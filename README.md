# Bright Coffee Shop Sales Analysis

## Business Analytics Case Study

Bright Coffee Shop Sales Analysis is a business analytics case study focused on understanding sales performance, identifying revenue drivers and uncovering opportunities to improve product performance and overall revenue growth.

## Business Objective

Bright Coffee's CEO wants to grow the company's revenue and improve product performance.

The analysis focuses on understanding product revenue, trading-period performance, sales trends across products and time, transaction behaviour and opportunities to improve sales performance.

## Key Insights

- Bright Coffee generated **R698.8K in revenue** from **149,116 transactions** and **214,470 units sold**.
- Revenue increased from **R81.7K in January to R166.5K in June**, representing **103.8% growth**.
- **Coffee** is the largest revenue category, contributing approximately **38.6%** of total revenue.
- **Coffee and Tea together contribute approximately 66.7% of revenue**, making them the core revenue categories.
- Average transaction value is **R4.69**, with an average selling price of **R3.26 per unit**.
- Customers purchase an average of **1.44 units per transaction**.
- Store revenue is relatively balanced, with Hell's Kitchen contributing **33.8%**, Astoria **33.2%** and Lower Manhattan **32.9%**.
- **09:00–12:00** is the strongest three-hour trading period, generating **R220.2K** in revenue.
- **18:00–21:00** is the weakest trading period, generating **R65.7K**.
- Revenue performance therefore varies across both **products and trading periods**, creating opportunities for targeted commercial interventions.

---

## Recommendations

Bright Coffee should focus on strengthening proven demand while testing targeted initiatives to improve transaction value and weaker trading periods.

Priorities include:

- Protecting product availability during peak trading periods
- Increasing transaction value through bundles and cross-selling
- Testing targeted offers during weaker trading periods
- Protecting the Coffee and Tea revenue base
- Reviewing underperforming products using revenue, volume and price together
- Measuring commercial interventions before scaling

**Understand → Identify → Act → Measure → Optimise**
